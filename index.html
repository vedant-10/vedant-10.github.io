01 // PROFILE

About me

A practical engineering approach connecting design documentation with on-site execution.

Electrical design and project coordination

I have nearly two years of experience supporting electrical project delivery, coordinating with clients, suppliers and internal teams, and preparing engineering documentation. My work includes EHT power distribution panel design, Single Line Diagrams (SLDs), wiring diagrams, General Arrangement Drawings (GADs), cable calculations, cable schedules and material estimation.
